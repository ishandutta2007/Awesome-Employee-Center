## Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global Employee Experience Management (EXM) and digital workplace portal sector is valued between **$7.5 billion and $8.6 billion**, projected to surpass **$17.5 billion by the 2030s** at an estimated CAGR of ~9.5–10%. The sector is **moderately fragmented**: mega-cap enterprise suites (Microsoft Viva, ServiceNow Employee Center) act as powerful ecosystem anchors for enterprise workflows and communications, while specialized modern intranet and digital workplace vendors (Simpplr, Workvivo, Unily, LumApps) fiercely compete for mid-market and frontline engagement.

## Open-Source Employee Centers & Portals

Self-hosted, transparent, and customizable platforms to deploy internal portals, self-service desks, and digital workspaces for teams.

* [frappe/erpnext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers) — Comprehensive enterprise system featuring a full employee lifecycle portal, expense claims, attendance tracking, leave applications, and organizational hierarchy.
* [mattermost/mattermost](https://github.com/mattermost/mattermost) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mattermost/mattermost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/mattermost/mattermost/stargazers) — Secure collaboration and digital workplace communication platform for mission-critical operations, internal announcements, and automated workflow playbooks.
* [opf/openproject](https://github.com/opf/openproject) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opf/openproject?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/opf/openproject/stargazers) — Open-source team collaboration and project management hub featuring team dashboards, roadmap timelines, task management, and document collaboration.
* [frappe/hrms](https://github.com/frappe/hrms) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/hrms?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/hrms/stargazers) — Dedicated modern HRMS and employee self-service portal featuring geofenced check-ins, automated payroll, salary slips, leave approvals, and employee directory.
* [humhub/humhub](https://github.com/humhub/humhub) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/humhub/humhub?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/humhub/humhub/stargazers) — Flexible open-source enterprise social network and intranet solution providing team spaces, employee profiles, knowledge bases, activity feeds, and modular add-ons.
* [glpi-project/glpi](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/glpi-project/glpi?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi/stargazers) — Open-source IT asset management and service desk portal providing internal ticketing, employee issue reporting, and knowledge base self-service.
* [zammad/zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/zammad/zammad?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/zammad/zammad/stargazers) — Modern web-based help desk and internal employee support ticketing system with multi-channel support, knowledge management, and SLA tracking.
